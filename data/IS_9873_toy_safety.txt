IS 9873 (Part 1): Safety of Toys — Mechanical and Physical Properties

Clause 1: Scope
Applies to all toys intended for use by children under 14 years, whether or
not designed to be used exclusively for play.

Clause 4: General Requirements
4.1 Toys shall be free from sharp edges and points that could cause injury.
4.2 Small parts that could be swallowed are prohibited in toys intended for
children under 36 months, as determined by the small-parts cylinder test.

Clause 6: Marking
6.1 Every toy or its package shall carry the BIS Standard Mark and, where
relevant, an age-grading warning label.
6.2 Electrically operated toys shall additionally comply with IS 302 Part 2
Section 25 for toy safety.

Clause 8: Flammability
8.1 Toy materials shall not burn at a rate faster than 30 mm per second when
tested per the method in Annex C.
