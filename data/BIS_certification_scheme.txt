BIS-CERT: BIS Product Certification Scheme — Overview

Section 1: What Certification Covers
BIS product certification confirms that a product conforms to the relevant
Indian Standard. Certified products are permitted to carry the BIS Standard
Mark (ISI Mark).

Section 2: Types of Certification
2.1 ISI Mark Scheme — voluntary/mandatory certification under IS 2:2016,
covering most consumer and industrial products.
2.2 Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS) — mandatory for specified
electronics and IT products; registration (not a licence) is granted after
testing at a BIS-recognized lab.
2.3 Voluntary Certification — for products not under mandatory order, a
manufacturer may still apply for the ISI mark to build consumer trust.

Section 3: Application Process
3.1 Applications are filed online via the BIS Care / Manak Online portal.
3.2 A factory inspection and sample testing at a BIS-recognized laboratory
follow the application.
3.3 On satisfactory results, a licence (ISI scheme) or registration (CRS)
is granted, permitting use of the mark.

Section 4: Fees and Validity
4.1 Licences are typically granted for one to two years and are renewable.
4.2 Fee structure includes a one-time application fee, annual marking fee,
and testing charges, as notified by BIS from time to time.
