IS 2347: Specification for Pressure Cookers (Domestic)

Clause 1: Scope
This standard covers requirements for aluminium and stainless steel domestic
pressure cookers of capacity 1 to 20 litres.

Clause 5: Material
5.1 Body and lid shall be made of aluminium alloy or stainless steel of food
grade quality.

Clause 7: Safety Devices
7.1 Every pressure cooker shall be fitted with at least three safety
devices: an operating vent/whistle, a safety valve, and a gasket-release
system.
7.2 The safety valve shall function at a pressure not exceeding 1.4 kg/cm2
above the operating pressure.

Clause 9: Marking
9.1 Each cooker shall bear the BIS Standard Mark, capacity in litres,
manufacturer's name, and month/year of manufacture.

Clause 12: Testing
12.1 Every batch shall undergo a hydraulic pressure test at 2.5 times the
working pressure before dispatch.
