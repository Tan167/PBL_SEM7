IS 14543: Packaged Natural Mineral Water and Packaged Drinking Water — Specification

Clause 1: Scope
This standard prescribes the requirements, methods of sampling, and test for
packaged drinking water intended for direct human consumption.

Clause 4: Requirements
4.1 The water shall be colourless, odourless, and free from suspended matter.
4.2 pH value shall be between 6.5 and 8.5.
4.3 Total dissolved solids shall not exceed 500 mg/l for packaged drinking
water (other than mineral water).

Clause 6: Packing and Marking
6.1 Every package shall be marked with the BIS Standard Mark under a licence
from the Bureau of Indian Standards.
6.2 Marking shall include batch or lot number, date of manufacture, and
best-before date.
6.3 Manufacturing unit's FSSAI licence number shall also be displayed.

Clause 9: Sampling and Testing
9.1 Samples shall be drawn as per IS 14543 Annex A and tested for
microbiological and chemical parameters before release of a batch.
