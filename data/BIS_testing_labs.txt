BIS-LAB: Recognized Testing Laboratories

Section 1: BIS Own Laboratories
BIS operates its own testing laboratories in several regions, including
Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Guwahati, each
equipped to test products against specific groups of Indian Standards.

Section 2: BIS-Recognized Outside Laboratories
2.1 In addition to its own labs, BIS recognizes independent third-party
laboratories that meet accreditation requirements (typically NABL
accreditation) to test against specific Indian Standards.
2.2 A list of recognized outside laboratories, by product/standard, is
published and updated on the BIS website.

Section 3: How to Locate a Lab for a Given Standard
3.1 Applicants can identify the nearest recognized laboratory for their
product's standard via the BIS regional office or the Manak Online portal's
lab-locator feature.
3.2 Only test reports from a BIS-recognized laboratory are accepted for
certification/licensing purposes.
