IS 3196 (Part 1): LPG Cylinders for Domestic and Commercial Use

Clause 1: Scope
Covers welded steel LPG cylinders of water capacity 5 kg to 50 kg for
domestic and commercial cooking use.

Clause 6: Design and Construction
6.1 Cylinders shall be manufactured from steel sheet conforming to IS 6240.
6.2 Minimum wall thickness shall be calculated as per Annex B based on
design pressure of not less than 25.5 kg/cm2.

Clause 10: Testing
10.1 Every cylinder shall undergo hydrostatic stretch testing before being
approved for filling.
10.2 Cylinders shall be re-tested every 5 years (for cylinders under 10
years old) or every 2.5 years (for older cylinders).

Clause 13: Marking
13.1 Cylinders shall be stamped with the BIS Standard Mark, water capacity,
tare weight, and date of manufacture and test.
