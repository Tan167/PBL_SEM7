BIS-HUID: Hallmarking of Gold and Silver Jewellery

Section 1: What is Hallmarking
Hallmarking is the BIS certification of the purity of gold and silver
jewellery and artefacts sold in India. It has been mandatory for gold
jewellery in notified categories since June 2021.

Section 2: HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification)
2.1 Every piece of hallmarked jewellery is engraved with a unique
alphanumeric HUID code during hallmarking at an Assaying & Hallmarking
Centre (AHC).
2.2 The HUID can be verified by consumers through the BIS Care app.

Section 3: Registration for Jewellers
3.1 Jewellers must register with BIS (online, via the BIS Care portal)
before selling hallmarked jewellery.
3.2 Registration is granted after document verification; no factory
inspection is required for jewellers (unlike product certification).

Section 4: Purity / Fineness Grades
4.1 Gold jewellery is hallmarked in the following caratage/fineness grades:
14K (585), 18K (750), 20K (833), 22K (916), 23K (958), 24K (999).
4.2 Silver articles are hallmarked as per IS 2112.
