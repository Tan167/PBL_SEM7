IS 302 (Part 1): Safety of Household and Similar Electrical Appliances

Clause 1: Scope
This standard applies to electrical appliances for household use with a rated
voltage not exceeding 250 V for single-phase appliances and 480 V for other
appliances.

Clause 5: Marking and Instructions
5.1 Appliances shall be marked with rated voltage, rated input power, and
manufacturer's name or trademark.
5.2 Class II appliances shall be marked with the double-insulation symbol.
5.3 Instructions for use shall be provided in English and Hindi, or in a
regional language as agreed with the purchaser.

Clause 8: Protection Against Access to Live Parts
8.1 Appliances shall be so constructed that there is adequate protection
against accidental contact with live parts.
8.2 Test: a standard test finger (IEC 61032, test probe B) shall not touch
live parts when inserted through any opening.

Clause 11: Heating
11.1 Appliances shall not attain excessive temperatures during normal use.
11.2 Temperature rise limits for external metal parts shall not exceed 30 K
above ambient for parts likely to be touched during normal handling.

Clause 22: Construction
22.1 Appliances shall be so constructed that the risk of fire is reduced,
even under fault conditions.
