BIS-LIC: Licensing Procedure for Manufacturers

Section 1: Application
1.1 Manufacturers apply online through the Manak Online / BIS Care portal,
selecting the relevant Indian Standard for their product.
1.2 Required documents include factory details, product test reports (if
available), and manufacturing process details.

Section 2: Factory Inspection
2.1 A BIS officer inspects the manufacturing unit to verify in-house
quality control and testing capability against the standard's requirements.

Section 3: Sample Testing
3.1 Samples are drawn during inspection and tested at a BIS-recognized
laboratory (in-house, BIS, or third-party recognized lab) against the
relevant Indian Standard.

Section 4: Grant of Licence and Validity
4.1 On satisfactory inspection and test results, BIS grants a licence
permitting use of the Standard Mark on the product.
4.2 Licences are typically valid for one to two years.

Section 5: Renewal
5.1 Renewal applications should be filed before expiry, along with the
renewal fee; a fresh inspection may be conducted periodically.
